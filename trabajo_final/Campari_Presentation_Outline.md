# Oral Presentation Outline — Campari Group Final Paper
### CON 34122 · Control y Gestión de Costos · Session 12 (10–12 min) · Individual

> Speaker notes for a ~12-slide deck. Timing in brackets. Goal: present the most relevant points of the analysis and the proposals — not read the paper.

---

**Slide 1 — Title [0:30]**
Integrated Thinking, ESG Costs and the Levers of Control at **Campari Group**.
Name · ITAM · Prof. Garciacano · June 2026. *Option C — public-reporting company.*

**Slide 2 — Why Campari? [1:00]**
- World's 6th premium spirits player; €3,070m net sales, 57.6% gross margin (FY2024).
- The hook: **~93% of its carbon footprint is Scope 3** (glass + agriculture) — a cost its P&L never shows. Perfect case for the three threads of the course.
- One sentence: *strong belief in sustainability, but the cost system can't see its most material ESG cost.*

**Slide 3 — The 6 Capitals at Campari [1:30]**
- Strongest: **Intellectual** (Aperol/Campari brands) & **Financial**.
- Most exposed: **Natural** (Scope 3 glass & agriculture) & **Social** (responsible drinking).
- Visual: the **"Iceberg of Costs"** — financial above the waterline; the biggest submerged block = Scope 3 carbon in glass.

**Slide 4 — Current cost system & the gap [1:00]**
- Hybrid process/batch manufacturer; ~42% of sales is COGS, **glass-led materials dominate**, low direct-labour share.
- That is *exactly* the Session-8 condition where volumetric overhead allocation **distorts** product costs.
- Campari pays the **tariff** for water/energy; it never books the **real cost** of ~820 kt Scope 3 CO₂e.

**Slide 5 — Levers of Control diagnosis [1:30]**
Bar chart, strongest → weakest:
- **Belief = STRONG** (Passion/Integrity/Pragmatism/Together; Code of Ethics 2004; lived, not decorative).
- **Interactive = EMERGING.**
- **Boundary = MODERATE** (no validated SBTi; supply-chain limits under-formalised).
- **Diagnostic-ESG = WEAK ← the core gap.**
- *The gap between strong Belief and weak Diagnostic = the firm's main transition/reputational risk.*

**Slide 6 — Distortion: cross-subsidisation [1:00]**
- 50+ brands, Aperol (high volume) → craft tail (low volume).
- Hypothesis: aperitifs **over-absorb** overhead, craft tail **under-absorbs** → flagship looks *less* profitable than it is.
- Illustrative ABC shift €18m (labelled assumption). *"ABC redistributes, it doesn't invent cost."*

**Slide 7 — Relevant costs: the Novi Ligure decision [1:00]**
- Real 2024 decision: **~€75m to double Aperol capacity.**
- Make vs. buy: make saves ~€5m (illustrative) — but **opportunity cost** + **sunk-cost discipline** refine it.
- ESG twist: making keeps it in clean **Scope 1+2**; the cost answer and the carbon answer **agree** — only the integrated view sees it.

**Slide 8 — Materiality: where to act [1:00]**
- Double-materiality table: **Scope 3 / glass & agriculture = HIGH financial AND HIGH impact** → most material, most urgent.
- Contrast with the course's Disney cases (Scope 1+2 dominated) — at a brand-builder, the action is in **Scope 3**.

**Slide 9 — Proposals by lever (1) [1:00]**
- **Belief (reinforce):** time-bound Scope-3 purpose; frame decarbonisation as *Integrity* + *Together*.
- **Boundary (fix):** validate **SBTi**; glass-carbon ceiling, recycled-content floor with budgets; supplier ESG limits (GRI 308/414, SASB).

**Slide 10 — Proposals by lever (2): the core fix [1:30]**
- **Diagnostic (weakest):**
  1. Pilot **ABC + ABC Ambiental** → **carbon-adjusted margin per House** (the number Campari doesn't compute).
  2. Put **3–5 ESG CPVs** on the monthly dashboard *next to EBITDA*, reviewed **by exception**.
- **Interactive:** quarterly strategic ESG review; carbon-price scenarios (€40/€80/€200).

**Slide 11 — Roadmap [1:00]**
- **0–6m:** ESG CPVs live; ABC pilot; purpose statement.
- **6–18m:** carbon-adjusted margins; SBTi; boundary budgets.
- **18m–3y:** group-wide ABC; internal carbon price; quarterly interactive review.
- **3–5y:** carbon-adjusted P&L by House; CSRD/ESRS + IFRS S1/S2.

**Slide 12 — Close [0:30]**
*From a report to a management practice: a Campari that can cost, steer and report the carbon in every bottle — building iconic brands AND superior returns, integrated.*
